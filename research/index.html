Bio

Background, education, and current research direction.

I am Yuqian Zhang, a first-year MScAC student at the University of Toronto. My work sits at the intersection of mathematical structure, learning systems, and physical modeling.

My research centers on machine learning, mathematical reasoning, computational physics and biology, and computer graphics. I have worked on spatial transcriptomics denoising with graph variational auto-encoders, ocean surface representation learning with transformers, Neural-ODE-based gene expression field estimation, and Bayesian-PINN-Kalman hybrids for time-series inverse problems, alongside an independent study of diffusion posterior sampling for general inverse problems.
